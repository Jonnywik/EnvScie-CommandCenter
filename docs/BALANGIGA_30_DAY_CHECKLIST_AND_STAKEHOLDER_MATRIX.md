# Balangiga Command Center: 30-Day Operational Checklist and Stakeholder Mapping Matrix

**Use.** This working document turns the initial rollout phase into a day-by-day municipal operating checklist. It is designed for a **training-only, LGU-owned pilot**. It must not be used to authorize live public warnings, live SMS delivery, native push delivery, or emergency dispatch until the separate readiness gates in the implementation plan are formally approved.

**Planning basis.** Balangiga is a municipal-scale setting with a reported 2024 population of 14,473, substantial provincial poverty context, and documented exposure to typhoons, storm surge, and flooding. The first month should therefore validate people, processes, power, connectivity, and community inclusion before adding more technology or partner access.[1] [2] [3]

> **Day-30 success is not a live-service launch.** It is a documented decision that the LGU can proceed, pause, or revise the controlled pilot with clear ownership, fallback procedures, and partner boundaries.

## 1. Operating Rules for the First 30 Days

| Rule | Required practice | Evidence to retain |
|---|---|---|
| LGU ownership | The MDRRMO/Municipal DRRMO is the system owner; the Mayor or authorized official is the executive sponsor. | Signed project charter and role matrix. |
| Training-only posture | Use clearly labelled demo, training, or exercise records. No public warning, emergency dispatch, live SMS, or push message is sent from the pilot. | Exercise-data label; activation log showing no live send. |
| Position-based continuity | Use named people for the pilot, but document roles and alternates by office/position. | Duty roster, alternates list, handover template. |
| Partner minimum access | Partners receive no system access by default. Start with briefings, workshops, and aggregated readiness information. | Partner engagement register and access decision log. |
| Privacy by design | Treat SOS, location, health, and household details as restricted. Do not share screenshots or records through informal chat. | Data inventory, access matrix, approved communication channels. |
| Barangay inclusion | Select pilot barangays with transparent criteria and include diverse groups in feedback sessions. | Selection scorecard, attendance, anonymized feedback summary. |
| Sustainability test | Do not commit to an operating function that cannot be sustained by the LGU after short-term project support ends. | Baseline cost worksheet and decision note. |

## 5. Day-30 Readiness Scorecard

The steering group should rate each item as **Green** (evidence complete), **Amber** (partly complete; controlled remediation), or **Red** (stop condition). A Day-30 “proceed” decision requires no unresolved red item in governance, privacy, staffing, or fallback.

| Dimension | Green evidence | Amber condition | Red stop condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ownership and authority | Signed charter, roles, alternates, approval map. | One noncritical role unresolved with interim plan. | No accountable LGU owner or unclear warning/dispatch authority. |
| Data protection | Data inventory, least-privilege accounts, access log, sharing rules. | Minor documentation gap with owner/date. | Shared accounts, uncontrolled case sharing, or no privacy focal person. |
| Community inclusion | Transparent pilot selection and feedback influenced design. | One group not yet reached but session scheduled. | Pilot selection is opaque or excludes key affected groups. |
| Technical continuity | Training environment, backup/paper fallback tested. | Noncritical technical defect with workaround. | No power/connectivity fallback or inability to identify exercise data. |
| Partner governance | Due diligence and role note for every partner; no unauthorized access. | MoU under legal review with no access granted. | Partner is promised access without purpose, agreement, or safeguards. |
| Exercise performance | Staff distinguish receipt, acknowledgement, dispatch, and arrival; after-action actions assigned. | Some process delays with remediation. | Unsafe misinformation, unauthorized action, or privacy breach. |
| Sustainability | Basic cost categories, LGU ownership, next-90-day plan. | Funding question under review with phased scope. | Core function depends on unconfirmed temporary funding. |
