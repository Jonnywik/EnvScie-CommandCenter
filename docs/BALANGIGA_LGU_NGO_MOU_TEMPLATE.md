# Template Memorandum of Understanding
## Between the Municipality of Balangiga, Eastern Samar and [Name of NGO/CSO/Partner]
### For a Scoped Command Center Readiness and Disaster-Risk-Reduction Partnership

> **Important legal notice.** This is a working operational template, not legal advice and not a signed instrument. Before use, the Municipality of Balangiga should have this document reviewed by its authorized legal office, procurement/finance authorities where applicable, data-protection officer or privacy focal person, and the partner’s authorized legal representative. The parties must complete all bracketed fields and remove clauses that do not apply. ## MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING

This Memorandum of Understanding (“**MoU**”) is made and entered into on **[date]**, in **[place]**, by and between:

**THE MUNICIPALITY OF BALANGIGA**, a local government unit of the Republic of the Philippines, with principal office at **[address]**, represented in this act by **[name and official position]**, duly authorized for this purpose, hereinafter referred to as the “**LGU**”; and

**[FULL LEGAL NAME OF NGO/CSO/PARTNER]**, a **[non-stock, non-profit / civil-society organization / humanitarian organization / other legal status]**, with principal office at **[address]**, represented in this act by **[name and official position]**, duly authorized for this purpose, hereinafter referred to as the “**Partner**.”

The LGU and the Partner are collectively referred to as the “**Parties**.”

### RECITALS

WHEREAS, Republic Act No. 10121 promotes a comprehensive, integrated, proactive, transparent, and participatory approach to disaster risk reduction and management, including the participation of civil society, private actors, volunteers, and communities;[1]

WHEREAS, the LGU is establishing a **training-first Command Center readiness pilot** to support verified information handling, evacuation-center readiness, SOS workflow exercises, community participation, and accountable coordination;

WHEREAS, the Parties recognize that the Command Center does not transfer LGU authority over public warnings, emergency dispatch, personal data, or statutory DRRM responsibilities to the Partner;

WHEREAS, the Parties intend to cooperate only within a defined scope that protects residents, respects local governance, and follows applicable privacy, safeguarding, procurement, financial, and other laws;

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

---

## Article 1. Purpose and Guiding Principles

### 1.1 Purpose

The purpose of this MoU is to establish a **limited and purpose-specific partnership** through which the Partner may support the LGU’s Command Center readiness activities in the following approved area(s):

| Select applicable scope | Description | Initial outcome |
|---|---|---|
| [ ] Community preparedness and outreach | Inclusive community awareness, feedback, and readiness sessions. | Validated resident journeys and outreach plan. |
| [ ] Anticipatory action | Forecast-informed readiness triggers, checklists, and pre-agreed actions. | Scoped readiness trigger and drill design. |
| [ ] First aid, health, or referral preparedness | Training, service mapping, referral protocol design. | Approved referral workflow and training schedule. |
| [ ] Evacuation-center readiness | Facility verification, inclusion/accessibility review, readiness exercises. | Updated readiness records with an LGU owner. |
| [ ] Volunteer capacity | Training, role definition, supervised exercise participation. | Documented, bounded volunteer role. |
| [ ] Logistics or resource coordination | Non-identifiable resource-offer registry and readiness mapping. | Resource coordination procedure. |
| [ ] Technical mentoring or training | Non-production coaching, drills, accessibility review, or documentation. | Training report and acceptance criteria. |
| [ ] Other | **[Specify clearly.]** | **[Specify.]** |

### 1.2 Guiding principles

The Parties shall implement this MoU according to the following principles:

1. **LGU authority and accountability.** The LGU retains responsibility for municipal DRRM decisions, public warnings, emergency dispatch, and the use of official records.
2. **Do no harm.** Activities shall not create a false impression of emergency coverage, response capability, safety clearance, or guaranteed aid.
3. **Community participation.** The Parties shall seek meaningful inclusion of women, children and youth as appropriate, older persons, persons with disabilities, farmers, fishers, and other locally relevant groups without requiring disclosure of sensitive personal information.
4. **Neutrality and non-partisanship.** The partnership shall not be used for partisan political activity, personal endorsement, discriminatory beneficiary selection, or preferential access based on political affiliation.
5. **Data minimization.** The Parties shall use the least information necessary to perform an approved task; aggregated or de-identified information is preferred.
6. **Transparency and auditability.** Roles, access, contributions, decisions, referrals, and exceptions shall be recorded and reviewable.
7. **Sustainability.** The Parties shall not create an essential function that depends solely on time-limited Partner funding or staff without an agreed LGU sustainability plan.

## Article 2. Definitions

For this MoU, the following terms apply:

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Command Center** | The LGU-owned operational platform and associated procedures used for training, readiness, verified information handling, and authorized coordination. |
| **Training-only pilot** | A controlled exercise environment using clearly labelled training or demo data. It does not authorize live public warnings, live provider sends, or real emergency dispatch. |
| **Personal information** | Information from which an individual may be identified directly or indirectly. |
| **Sensitive personal information** | Information including health, age, political affiliations, or other categories recognized under applicable Philippine law.[2] |
| **Identifiable case data** | Resident-specific SOS, location, health, family, household, incident, or referral information. |
| **Aggregated information** | Information summarized so that individual persons or households cannot reasonably be identified. |
| **Approved referral** | A case-specific transfer of limited information that is necessary for a documented service purpose, approved by the authorized LGU role, and lawful under applicable requirements. |
| **Partner focal person** | A named representative authorized by the Partner to receive official partnership communications. |
| **Public warning** | An alert or message issued to the public under the LGU’s approved source-verification and authority procedures. |

## Article 3. Roles, Authority, and Limits

### 3.1 LGU responsibilities

The LGU shall:

1. Designate a system owner from the MDRRMO/Municipal DRRMO and identify an executive sponsor, operations lead, public-information authority, technical custodian, privacy focal person, and barangay liaison.
2. Define the approved pilot boundary, operating posture, exercise rules, and activation criteria.
3. Retain authority for public warnings, verification and publication decisions, emergency dispatch, resident case management, and official records.
4. Provide the Partner with only the orientation, information, and access that are necessary for the approved scope.
5. Maintain an access log, issue register, decision record, and after-action review process for activities under this MoU.
6. Notify the Partner of changes in LGU focal persons, pilot status, or suspension decisions that affect the collaboration.

### 3.2 Partner responsibilities

The Partner shall:

1. Designate a primary and alternate focal person with authority to coordinate the approved scope.
2. Provide the skills, personnel, materials, training, logistics, or technical support explicitly listed in **Annex A**.
3. Follow LGU safety, safeguarding, inclusion, confidentiality, and access rules during any activity in Balangiga.
4. Refrain from issuing public warnings, representing itself as an LGU dispatcher, or making promises of aid, rescue, eligibility, or response time through the Command Center.
5. Refrain from requesting or retaining identifiable case data unless an approved referral process and appropriate agreement authorize it.
6. Promptly report any suspected misuse, unauthorized disclosure, safeguarding concern, conflict of interest, or data-security incident to the LGU focal person.
7. Participate in the after-action review and complete assigned corrective actions within agreed timelines.

### 3.3 Matters reserved to the LGU

The following may not be delegated to the Partner under this MoU unless a separate lawful written authority explicitly provides otherwise:

| Reserved matter | LGU control |
|---|---|
| Public warning issuance, modification, cancellation, or correction | Authorized LGU public-information and DRRM approval process. |
| Emergency dispatch decision or responder assignment confirmation | Authorized LGU operational chain. |
| Access to identifiable SOS, location, health, or household records | Authorized LGU role; only a documented, necessary referral may create a limited exception. |
| Determination of disaster declaration, local emergency posture, or official road/route clearance | Competent LGU or legally authorized government authority. |
| User/account administration and role assignment | LGU technical custodian with privacy oversight. |
| Official record retention and audit | LGU system owner and privacy focal person. |

## Article 4. Scope of Work and Deliverables

### 4.1 Work plan

The Parties shall complete a time-bound work plan in **Annex A** before operational activity begins. The work plan shall identify:

| Required field | Description |
|---|---|

| Activities | Specific workshops, drills, verification tasks, or support activities. |
| Locations and participants | Pilot barangays, facilities, and participant categories; do not list individual beneficiaries unless necessary and authorized. |
| LGU and Partner focal persons | Named primary and alternate contacts. |
| Inputs | Funding, staff time, equipment, training materials, logistics, or in-kind contributions. |
| Deliverables | Documents, training records, readiness checks, or after-action outputs. |
| Dates | Start, milestone, review, and completion dates. |
| Acceptance criteria | Evidence that the activity is complete and useful. |
| Risks and safeguards | Safety, privacy, inclusion, access, and continuity controls. |

### 4.2 No implied financial commitment

Unless Annex A expressly states otherwise and the Parties have completed all applicable approvals, this MoU does not create a financial obligation, procurement award, funding commitment, exclusive relationship, employment relationship, or authority to incur expense on behalf of the other Party.

### 4.3 Resource offers

All resource offers shall be documented in **Annex B** with the contributor, description, value if applicable, restrictions, operating costs, maintenance responsibility, acceptance decision, and exit/disposal plan. The LGU may decline any offer that creates unsustainable costs, unauthorized data access, or operational dependence.

## Article 5. Information Sharing, Privacy, and System Access

### 5.1 Data-governance status

For Command Center data, the LGU shall ordinarily act as the **personal information controller**. The Partner shall not receive personal information unless the Parties determine in writing that a specific approved activity requires it and that the lawful basis, safeguards, and responsibilities are documented.

The Parties acknowledge the Data Privacy Act principles of transparency, legitimate purpose, and proportionality.[2] The existence of a DRRM purpose does not authorize unrestricted data collection, sharing, retention, or reuse.

### 5.2 Default information tier

The default Partner access level is **aggregated, non-identifiable readiness information only**. The Partner may receive, as applicable:

| Permitted by default | Not permitted by default |
|---|---|
| Training calendar, aggregate readiness trends, centre-readiness tasks, non-identifiable resource needs, approved public bulletins, and Partner-specific work-plan updates. | Raw SOS feed, resident location, health details, family/household data, unredacted incident notes, system credentials, mass exports, or other sensitive personal information. |

### 5.3 Identifiable referral exception

If an activity requires an identifiable referral, the Parties shall complete an **Approved Referral Record** in Annex C or an equivalent LGU-approved form before transfer, except where a documented emergency/legal process makes prior documentation impossible. The record must state the minimum data, service purpose, recipient, authorizer, time, secure transfer method, retention date, and follow-up owner.

### 5.4 System access

No Partner user shall receive a Command Center account unless all of the following conditions are met:

1. The account is required by the approved scope and cannot be replaced with an aggregated report or supervised demonstration.
2. The user is named, trained, and assigned the least-privilege role.
3. The LGU technical custodian and privacy focal person approve the access in writing.
4. The account has an expiry date, audit logging, and deprovisioning instruction.
5. The Partner agrees not to share credentials, export data without permission, or use screenshots/unapproved channels.

### 5.5 Security and incident reporting

The Partner shall notify the LGU privacy focal person and system owner **within [X hours]** after becoming aware of a suspected unauthorized access, loss, disclosure, misdirection, or misuse of information under this MoU. The Parties shall preserve evidence, limit further disclosure, coordinate corrective action, and comply with applicable notification and investigation requirements.

### 5.6 Retention, return, and deletion

At the end of the approved activity or this MoU, the Partner shall return or securely delete information in accordance with the LGU-approved retention schedule and certify completion in writing, unless lawfully required to retain specific information. Aggregated learning material may be retained only if it cannot reasonably identify individuals or households and if the LGU authorizes retention.

---

## Article 6. Safeguarding, Inclusion, and Community Accountability

1. The Parties shall maintain safeguarding practices appropriate to children, older persons, persons with disabilities, survivors of gender-based violence, and other at-risk groups.
2. Participation in feedback, co-design, training, or drills shall be voluntary and shall not be conditioned on aid, political support, app installation, or personal-data disclosure beyond what is necessary.
3. The Parties shall provide accessible communication options where feasible, including plain-language explanations, local-language support as appropriate, non-smartphone alternatives, and accessible venue arrangements.
4. The Parties shall establish a feedback and complaint channel, publish the responsible focal point, and record how concerns are resolved.
5. The Partner shall not use images, names, case stories, or quotations from residents for publicity without the applicable consent and LGU approval processes.

---

## Article 7. Communications and Public Representation

1. All public communications relating to the Command Center pilot shall be accurate, source-aware, non-partisan, and approved through the LGU’s designated public-information process.
2. Neither Party shall represent the pilot as a live emergency-response service, guaranteed rescue mechanism, or fully deployed public-alert system unless the relevant function has separately passed the LGU’s operational gate.
3. Media releases, announcements, photographs, public presentations, and logo use shall be jointly cleared in writing by **[LGU focal person]** and **[Partner focal person]**.
4. The Partner may describe its own contribution accurately but shall not claim to own, operate, or control the municipal Command Center.

---

## Article 8. Monitoring, Learning, and Audit

### 8.1 Performance review

The Parties shall review the partnership at least **[monthly/quarterly]** during the term. Reviews shall address:

| Review area | Required question |
|---|---|
| Relevance | Is the Partner’s contribution addressing the approved local readiness gap? |
| Inclusion | Are pilot barangays and community groups participating fairly and safely? |
| Data protection | Did any access or sharing exceed the agreed scope? |
| Operational safety | Did the work create confusion about authority, warning, dispatch, or response? |
| Sustainability | Can the LGU maintain the resulting function or asset? |
| Learning | What will be retained, changed, paused, or ended? |

### 8.2 Records

The LGU shall maintain, and the Partner shall support, the following records: role matrix; work plan; training attendance; resource offers; access approvals; referral records; incident reports; after-action reviews; corrective-action register; and termination/deprovisioning evidence.

### 8.3 Right to suspend

The LGU may immediately suspend an activity, account, data exchange, or Partner presence in the Command Center if it reasonably believes there is a safety, privacy, safeguarding, authority, misinformation, conflict-of-interest, or operational-readiness risk. The Partner may request a review, but suspension remains effective until the LGU documents a safe resolution.

---

## Article 9. Conflict of Interest, Non-Exclusivity, and Anti-Corruption

1. The Parties shall disclose actual, potential, or perceived conflicts of interest connected to the scope, beneficiary selection, procurement, staffing, access, or funding.
2. This MoU is non-exclusive. The LGU may partner with other qualified organizations, and the Partner may support other communities, subject to confidentiality and non-conflict obligations.
3. Neither Party shall offer, solicit, accept, or use improper inducements in connection with activities under this MoU.
4. This MoU shall not be used to promote a political candidate, party, campaign, or private commercial product.

---

## Article 10. Term, Amendment, Suspension, and Termination

### 10.1 Term

This MoU takes effect upon signature by both Parties and remains valid until **[date]**, unless earlier terminated or extended in writing.

### 10.2 Amendment

Any amendment must be written, dated, and signed by authorized representatives of both Parties. A change in scope, data access, financial responsibility, public communication, or live-service status requires explicit review by the LGU system owner and privacy focal person.

### 10.3 Termination

Either Party may terminate this MoU by giving **[30] days’** written notice, unless immediate termination is necessary to protect residents, comply with law, respond to a serious safeguarding/privacy breach, or prevent material operational harm.

### 10.4 Exit obligations

Upon termination or expiry, the Parties shall complete Annex D: return or deletion of data; account deprovisioning; handover of approved outputs; disposition of resources; notification to affected focal persons; and final lessons/closure report.

---

## Article 11. Dispute Resolution and Governing Rules

The Parties shall first seek to resolve disagreements through good-faith discussion between the designated focal persons. If unresolved within **[15] days**, the matter shall be elevated to the LGU executive sponsor and the Partner’s authorized representative. Nothing in this clause prevents the LGU from taking immediate protective action under Article 8.3.

This MoU shall be interpreted consistently with applicable Philippine law, LGU policies, and the Parties’ lawful mandates. Where this template conflicts with a mandatory legal, regulatory, or formal local-government requirement, that requirement shall control.

---

## Article 12. Effect of the MoU

This MoU records the Parties’ cooperative understanding within the approved scope. It does not create an employment, agency, joint-venture, or procurement relationship; it does not transfer governmental authority; and it does not create a public representation that services are live or guaranteed.
