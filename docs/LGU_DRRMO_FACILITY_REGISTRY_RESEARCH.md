# Official Facility Registry Research — Balangiga

## Verified Government Sources

The Department of Health Eastern Visayas Center for Health Development publishes a regional list that includes the **Balangiga Rural Health Unit**. The Department of Health National Tuberculosis Control Program facility directory separately lists the **Albino M. Duran Memorial Hospital** as a public hospital at National Highway, Poblacion 6, Balangiga, Eastern Samar.[1] [2]

Google Maps resolves the hospital to the local Plus Code **494R+XGH, Balangiga** and the Rural Health Unit to **495P+PHX, Rizal Street, Balangiga**. These map-search results provide provisional display coordinates only and will be recorded separately from government registry provenance as requiring LGU location validation.

The DILG infrastructure-monitoring portal contains government project records for relevant evacuation-center and multi-purpose-hall facilities, including Balangiga-related projects. It is useful as supporting infrastructure context, but it is not being treated as a current operational-status or occupancy registry.[3]

## Initial Import Boundary

The first facility-registry release will import the two verified DOH health-facility records as **official-registry reference facilities**. The hospital’s government-published address can support a provisional map placement; the Rural Health Unit record lacks a precise published street address. Any coordinates derived from map search or geocoding will therefore be marked **LGU location verification required** rather than represented as an official coordinate.

> A registry listing confirms that a facility is recognized in the cited source. It does not confirm that it is open, staffed, accessible, equipped, or appropriate for an emergency task at the time of use.

## Access Constraints

The broader DOH National Health Facility Registry interface offers CSV and Excel exports but is currently protected by a CAPTCHA in this environment. The integration will not bypass that protection. The facility layer will use the official, publicly visible regional and program directories above until the registry export is authorized or a downloadable government feed is supplied.
