# Regional Weather Overlay Research Notes

**Research date:** 23 August 2026 (PHT)  
**Scope:** Decision-support overlays for an LGU Command Map during extreme typhoon events. These sources do not independently authorize an evacuation, route clearance, field deployment, or public warning.

| Source | Candidate layer | Evidence and intended use | Integration constraint |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [PAGASA Radar](https://www.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/radar) | Radar quantitative precipitation estimate (QPE) | PAGASA describes QPE as identifying and measuring rainfall levels after radar quality control. Use as the preferred Philippine observed-rainfall layer when a supported machine-readable or approved partner feed is available. | The public product page does not document a stable, reusable tile/API contract. Obtain written access terms or a feed agreement before production ingestion. |
| [PAGASA HIMAWARI 8/9](https://www.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/products-and-services/satellite) | Visible/infrared/water-vapour satellite loop | PAGASA identifies Himawari 8/9 as its most-used real-time imagery for forecasting and tropical-cyclone analysis, with 10-minute imagery. Use for cloud-top structure and system-scale situational awareness. | Prefer official PAGASA/JMA delivery with visible timestamp and product type. Satellite imagery is not a rainfall, wind-at-surface, flood-depth, or damage observation. |
| [PAGASA PANaHON](https://www.panahon.gov.ph/) | Station rainfall, wind, pressure, humidity and observation freshness | PAGASA’s network presents near-real-time automatic and manned station data and an ECMWF-backed forecast. Use as point observations, with age/quality markers, rather than interpolated municipal-wide certainty. | Confirm a stable access method and station coverage near Balangiga. Do not interpolate sparse stations into unverified road or barangay conditions. |
| [PAGASA storm-surge and wind products](https://www.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/) | Forecast storm surge, tropical-cyclone bulletins, severe-wind impact, shipping and rainfall products | These are the primary Philippine warning/impact products and should remain the authoritative label and alert source for local operation. | Some public pages expose product content but not a documented API. Ingest only permitted official data and preserve issuance time, source URL, and original warning geography. |
| [JAXA GPM/GSMaP](https://sharaku.eorc.jaxa.jp/GSMaP/) | Hourly satellite precipitation | A complementary wide-area precipitation layer for gaps in radar coverage and regional context. It should be visually distinct from local radar/QPE. | Satellite precipitation has latency, retrieval error and coarse resolution relative to municipal decision-making. Use an explicit satellite-derived label and not as a substitute for PAGASA rainfall warnings. |
| [JTWC](https://www.cnmoc.usff.navy.mil/Our-Commands/Fleet-Weather-Center-San-Diego/Joint-Typhoon-Warning-Center/) | Regional track, forecast cone/points, satellite image and advisory context | Public products include text, graphics, 3-hour position updates, KMZ, and satellite imagery for active systems. Use as a regional comparison layer. | JTWC itself directs users to consult the national meteorological agency for country- and locality-relevant products. PAGASA remains the authoritative Philippine warning source. |
| [Copernicus GloFAS](https://global-flood.emergency.copernicus.eu/general-information/about-glofas/) | Large-river flood forecast and satellite flood-monitoring context | GloFAS supplies daily global river-flood forecasts and Sentinel-1 based monitoring when imagery is available. Use as a supplementary regional early-warning context layer. | Registration is required. GloFAS explicitly does not provide real-time flash-flood, coastal-flood or inundation-area forecasts; it is not appropriate as a municipal flash-flood clearance tool. |
| Lightning network provider | Lightning-density / recent-strike overlay | Potentially useful for crew exposure awareness and thunderstorm escalation, if an authoritative, licensed data feed is procured. | Do not use unverified public scraping. A coverage, latency, licensing, and alerting protocol must be established first; no source is proposed for immediate integration. |

## Integration priority

The recommended order is: **(1)** approved PAGASA QPE/station/warning feeds; **(2)** PAGASA or JMA Himawari loop; **(3)** official PAGASA storm-surge, rainfall, and wind warning geography; **(4)** GSMaP as visibly satellite-derived regional precipitation context; **(5)** JTWC as an explicitly secondary regional comparison; and **(6)** GloFAS for large-river context only. A lightning feed should remain a separately procured capability rather than an improvised public-data integration.

All future overlay records should carry: provider, product, observed-versus-forecast classification, issue/observation time, ingest time, geographic resolution, age state, source URL, and a bounded operational-use statement.
