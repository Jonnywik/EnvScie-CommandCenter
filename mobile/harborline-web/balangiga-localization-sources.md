# Balangiga, Eastern Samar Localization Sources

- The [Provincial Government of Eastern Samar Balangiga page](https://easternsamarprovince.com/cityandmunicipalities/balangiga/) identifies Balangiga as an Eastern Samar locality and publishes provincial emergency contacts: Provincial Disaster Risk Reduction Management Office **0917 117 0807**, Bureau of Fire Protection **0917 182 4461**, Provincial Police Station **0947 396 1530** / **0956 349 7527**, and Provincial Hospital **0915 915 0764** / **0905 894 5900**. Accessed 22 August 2026.
- The [Climate Change Commission RAPID program page](https://climate.gov.ph/our-programs/foreign-assisted-project-management-system/resilience-and-preparedness-towards-inclusive-development) states that Balangiga was among the Yolanda-affected Eastern Samar municipalities covered by the 2014–2019 RAPID program, which focused on risk and vulnerability assessment, emergency preparedness and response, and climate/disaster risk reduction. Accessed 22 August 2026.
