03 · System architecture

A layered architecture keeps critical actions local.

The platform separates resident experience, resilient transport, municipal services, and spatial intelligence—so an outage in one layer does not erase the information or action needed in another.

System of record · route intelligence · realtime operations
Operating planes
Resident edgeReact Native / Expo, offline cache, SOS outbox, emergency toolkit.
Resilient serviceHTTPS, WebSocket, compact signed SMS, FastAPI APIs and events.
Municipal corePostgreSQL + PostGIS, hazard logic, safe routing, Next.js command center.
Architecture principle: every critical action has a local store, a trusted service path, and an operational handoff.
