07 · Operational cycle

The response loop is designed for the first critical minutes.

The command center turns a raw report into a safer next action by linking verification, location, routing, dispatch, and continuous operational updates.

Operating rule: let the newest verified hazard information constrain every routing and dispatch decision.
01

Verify

Confirm the alert source and current hazard layer before acting on it.

02

Locate

Read coordinates, delivery channel, and location confidence from the SOS report.

03

Route

Choose a safer path and center while excluding active hazard intersections.

04

Dispatch

Assign the appropriate response and make the incident status visible to the team.

05

Reconcile

Fold new reports, shelter capacity, and field updates back into the shared view.
