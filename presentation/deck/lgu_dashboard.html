06 · LGU command center
Shared operational picture
Implemented responsive dashboard · demo mode

The LGU dashboard turns incoming signals into a triage queue.

The command center gives responders one scanable view of the situation, then makes the next dispatch action explicit.

Situation metricsUntriaged SOS, critical alerts, open centers, and residents at risk.
Live situation mapHazard overlays, SOS markers, evacuation centers, and route context.
Priority SOS queueSeverity, age, channel, confidence, and current triage status in one scan.
Triage drawerAcknowledge, dispatch, resolve, inspect coordinates, and preserve incident history.
Verified alert feedOfficial or LGU-published updates remain distinct from community reports.
Operational rule: incident urgency and information trust are both visible before a responder is dispatched.
