04 · Resilient communications

Offline SOS turns a weak signal into an actionable record.

The emergency request is reduced to the smallest useful packet, stored locally, and delivered through the best available channel. The LGU sees how it arrived before making a dispatch decision.

Offline is a mode, not an error.When the network returns, the same incident can reconcile against the live service.

01

Capture the minimum

Identity, emergency type, coordinates, time, and a short message create the usable incident record.

02

Encode and protect

Coordinate quantization, compact encoding, CRC validation, and a shared secret support a one-SMS handoff.

03

Persist the outbox

The device retains the request through disruption and submits by internet or SMS when a channel is available.

04

Expose the channel

The LGU queue preserves whether the record arrived by internet, SMS, or mesh for informed triage.

Operational safeguard: SMS is a resilient delivery fallback; the system records integrity checks and does not assume delivery certainty.
