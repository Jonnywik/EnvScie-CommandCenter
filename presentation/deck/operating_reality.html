01 · Context

The operating reality is fragmented during a crisis.

When storms intensify, the same conditions that raise risk also disrupt information, movement, and communications. The response system must remain useful as the network becomes less reliable.

Coastal response conditions
01

Risk changes locally

Typhoon rainfall, storm surge, and flash flooding create different conditions across barangays and travel routes.

02

Connectivity can fail first

Power loss and interrupted signal delay residents’ access to updates and make online-only SOS channels unreliable.

03

Responders need one picture

Hazards, shelter status, verified alerts, and assistance requests must be reconciled before dispatch decisions are made.

Design consequence: build a local response loop—not merely another alert feed.
