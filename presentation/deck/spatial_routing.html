05 · Spatial decisioning

Spatial intelligence routes people away from active flood zones.

The routing service applies a simple but vital safety constraint: a road segment that intersects an active flood polygon does not remain eligible for the route.

Explainable by design: the command view can show the active hazard, the excluded crossings, and the selected path to an open evacuation center.
